Wi-Fi Alliance Passphrase Standards

  • Minimum Length: WPA2/WPA3 pre-shared keys require at least 8 characters, but 16+ characters are strongly recommended to defeat PBKDF2 dictionary attacks.
  • WPA3 SAE Advantage: WPA3 utilizes Simultaneous Authentication of Equals (Dragonfly handshake), preventing offline dictionary cracking even if weak passwords are used.
  • Entropy Factor: Passphrases containing mixed case, numbers, and special symbols provide over 80+ bits of entropy, rendering brute-force attacks computationally impossible.
  • Avoid Default Router SSIDs & Passwords: Factory default credentials for routers are often generated via known vendor algorithms.
